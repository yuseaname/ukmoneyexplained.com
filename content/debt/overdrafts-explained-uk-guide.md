> **Quick answer:** A UK bank overdraft lets you spend more than is in your account, up to an agreed limit. Since FCA rule changes in 2020, overdrafts are charged as a single annual interest rate (typically **35-40% APR**) — daily and monthly fees were abolished. Overdrafts are one of the most expensive forms of borrowing. To clear yours: switch to a 0% overdraft account, use a 0% balance transfer card, or pay it down £50/month. Source: [FCA — Overdrafts](https://www.fca.org.uk/consumers/overdrafts).

**Overdrafts Explained UK**

An overdraft can be a useful safety net or an expensive debt trap. This guide explains how UK overdrafts work, what they cost, and how to escape them.

## What Is an Overdraft?

An overdraft is a facility on your current account that lets you borrow money when your balance reaches zero (or below). There are two types:

### Arranged Overdraft
- Pre-agreed with your bank
- You have a set limit (e.g., £500 or £1,500)
- Charged at a single interest rate (APR)

### Unarranged Overdraft
- You spend beyond your balance without an agreed facility
- Or you exceed your arranged limit
- Same interest rate applies (since FCA reforms)
- Banks can no longer charge higher fees for unarranged vs arranged

## How Much Do Overdrafts Cost?

Since the **FCA overdraft reforms in April 2020**, all overdrafts are charged as a single annual interest rate:

| Bank | Typical Overdraft APR (2026) |
|------|-----------------------------|
| High street banks (Barclays, Lloyds, HSBC) | 35–39.9% |
| Digital banks (Monzo, Starling) | 15–35% |
| Building societies | 25–35% |

**Example:** A continuous £500 overdraft at 39.9% APR costs approximately **£199.50 per year** in interest — that's £16.63/month just in charges.

**Before the FCA reforms**, banks charged up to £90/month in daily fees — the reforms saved consumers over £1 billion annually.

Source: [FCA — Overdraft reforms](https://www.fca.org.uk/news/speeches/overdraft-pricing-reforms).

## Why Overdrafts Are Expensive

At 39.9% APR, overdrafts are:

- **More expensive than most credit cards** (typically 18-25% APR)
- **More expensive than personal loans** (typically 6-15% APR)
- **Comparable to payday loans** (which are now capped at 0.8%/day)
- **Harder to clear** because interest is charged daily and compounds

## How to Clear Your Overdraft

### 1. Switch to a 0% Overdraft Account
Several banks offer **interest-free overdraft buffers** for new customers:
- First Direct: £250 interest-free
- Nationwide: 12 months at 0% for switchers
- Starling: No interest on small overdrafts

Use the [Current Account Switch Service](https://www.currentaccountswitch.co.uk/) — it takes 7 days and switches everything automatically.

### 2. Use a 0% Money Transfer Credit Card
Some credit cards let you transfer money to your current account at 0% interest for 12-24 months. This clears the overdraft and gives you time to repay interest-free.

Read our [balance transfer credit cards guide](/balance-transfer-credit-cards-uk/) for how this works.

### 3. Pay It Down Gradually
- Treat your overdraft as a debt (because it is)
- Budget £50-100/month toward reducing it
- Ask your bank to **reduce your overdraft limit** as you pay it down — this prevents re-dipping
- Use our [budget planner](/budget-planner-uk/) to find the money

### 4. Ask for Help
If you're permanently in your overdraft and can't see a way out:
- Contact your bank — they must treat you fairly under FCA rules
- Ask about **structured repayment plans**
- Contact [StepChange](https://www.stepchange.org/) for free debt advice
- Read our [debt management plans guide](/debt-management-plans-uk-guide/)

## Your Overdraft Rights

Under FCA rules, banks must:

- Send you an **annual statement** showing total overdraft costs
- Provide **alert notifications** when you're near or over your limit
- Offer **tailored support** if you're in financial difficulty
- Not use overdrafts as a **trap** — they must help you reduce reliance

Source: [FCA — Overdraft help](https://www.fca.org.uk/consumers/overdrafts).

## Frequently Asked Questions

{{< faq "How much does a UK overdraft cost per month?" >}}
At the typical 39.9% APR, a £500 overdraft costs about £16.63/month in interest. A £1,000 overdraft costs about £33/month. The longer you stay in overdraft, the more it compounds.
{{< /faq >}}

{{< faq "Can I switch banks with an overdraft?" >}}
Yes. Use the Current Account Switch Service (CASS). Your new bank will either match your overdraft or offer a lower one. Some banks offer 0% overdrafts to attract switchers. The switch is guaranteed and takes 7 days.
{{< /faq >}}

{{< faq "Are unarranged overdraft fees banned?" >}}
Since April 2020, banks cannot charge higher fees for unarranged overdrafts than arranged ones. All overdrafts are charged at a single APR. Fixed daily or monthly fees are also banned.
{{< /faq >}}

{{< faq "How do I get out of my overdraft permanently?" >}}
Switch to a 0% overdraft account or use a 0% money transfer card to clear it, then budget to stay out. Ask your bank to reduce your limit gradually so you can't accidentally re-dip. Read our how to get out of debt guide for the full strategy.
{{< /faq >}}
