UK Money Explained is a free, independent resource. To keep it running we earn revenue through advertising and, where relevant, affiliate partnerships. This page explains how that works — and how it does not affect our content.

## Advertising

Where ads are shown, they are served by third-party networks (such as Google AdSense). Ads are:

- Labelled as advertisements;
- Selected by the ad network, not by us;
- Not endorsements by UK Money Explained.

## Affiliate links

Some links to products or services may be affiliate links. If you click one and take an action (for example, open an account or buy a product), we may earn a commission at no additional cost to you. Affiliate relationships may include financial products and services relevant to UK consumers.

Where a link is an affiliate link and it is practical to mark it, we do. Otherwise, this page is our standing disclosure.

## Editorial independence

Revenue does not decide what we write or how we rate anything:

- Advertisers and affiliate partners have no input on article topics, conclusions, or rankings;
- They cannot review or approve content before publication;
- They cannot suppress negative coverage.

We only recommend products and services we believe are genuinely useful, based on research and publicly available information.

## Not financial advice

Nothing on UK Money Explained constitutes financial advice. Content is general information for educational purposes. Your circumstances are unique — seek qualified advice (for example, from an FCA-authorised adviser) before making financial decisions. See also our [Editorial Policy](/editorial-policy/).
